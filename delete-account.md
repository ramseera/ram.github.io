
# Delete Account

## Overview
This page explains how to permanently delete your account and associated data.

## Before You Delete

Please note that deleting your account is **permanent and irreversible**. Consider these points:

- All your data will be removed
- You cannot recover your account or data after deletion
- Your username will become available for others to use

## Steps to Delete Your Account

1. Log in to your account
2. Navigate to **Settings** → **Account**
3. Scroll to the bottom and click **Delete Account**
4. Enter your password to confirm
5. Review the warning message carefully
6. Click **Permanently Delete** to confirm

## After Deletion

- Your account will be removed within 24 hours
- All associated data, including posts and settings, will be deleted
- You will receive a confirmation email
